Zero Skeuomorphism

Suprematism does not illustrate. It does not decorate. It creates a new reality where a black square on a field of white is not an icon of a box—it is the birth of autonomous form.

“I have broken the blue boundary of color limits, I have come out into the white; beside me, comrade pilots, swim in this infinity.” — Kazimir Malevich, Suprematist Manifesto

In practical web UI, the Zero Degree translates into uncompromised negative space, strict monospaced data readouts, hairline 1px bounding lines, and a solitary black or red anchor that commands the entire viewport.

Suprematist Design System

Unlike Supergraphics (which relies on warm earthy stripes and 70s corporate nostalgia) or Brutalism (which embraces intentional roughness and raw HTML default forms), Suprematism is an avant-garde pursuit of non-objective geometric purity and dynamic spatial equilibrium.

Chromatic Trinity

  • Vermilion Red: Dynamic Vector
  • Cobalt Blue: Spatial Counterweight
  • Cadmium Yellow: Focal Tension
